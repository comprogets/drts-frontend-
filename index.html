About the System

Web-based Document Request and Tracking System

Nova Schola Tanauan Inc. · Zone 4, Barangay Sambat, Tanauan City

Why this system exists

Requesting school documents the traditional way meant filling out paper forms, waiting in line at the Registrar's Office, and following up in person or by email just to check a request's progress. That process was slow, hard to monitor, and prone to misplaced or disorganized records — especially as the number of requests grew. This system replaces those manual steps with a single online platform where students, teachers, and school personnel can submit, process, and monitor document requests from anywhere, at any time.

What students and teachers can do

Create an account
Register once with your ID number, contact details, and school email — your Master File is used for every future request.
Request a document
Pick the department (Registrar, Academic Department, or Accounting), choose the exact document you need, and submit your purpose and details through a guided step-by-step form.
Pay the processing fee online
Settle the document fee via e-wallet or bank transfer and attach your reference code for verification.
Track the request live
Follow your request through every stage — Submitted, Approved, Processing, Release, Received — without visiting the office.
Get notified of updates
See status changes and admin notes as soon as your request is reviewed, approved, or released.
Access released documents
Download the final attached document once the Registrar marks your request complete.

What admin personnel can do

Registrar, Academics, and Accounting staff each sign in through a dedicated Admin Portal and see only the requests relevant to their role: the Registrar manages and processes requests end-to-end and can accept, reject, or update their status; the Academic Department reviews requests tied to academic records such as transcripts and grades; and Accounting verifies proof of payment before a request can move forward. This keeps every request accountable, routed to the right office, and visible in one shared system instead of scattered logbooks and email threads.

The goal

To make document requests faster, more transparent, and less dependent on repeated office visits — saving time for students and teachers, and reducing the manual workload, errors, and misplaced records that come with paper-based processing for the school's administrative staff.

Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started
Who can use this system?

Enrolled students, alumni, and teachers of Nova Schola Tanauan Inc. Students and alumni sign in as "Student," and teachers sign in as "Teacher," from the sign-in page.

How do I submit a request?

Sign in, then open "New Request." You'll choose a department, confirm your details, pick the document(s) you need, settle payment if required, and review before submitting.

Can I request more than one document at once?

Yes — you can select multiple documents from the same department within a single request.

Payments & Fees
How do I pay the document fee?

Pay via e-wallet or bank transfer to the school's official account, then enter the reference/OR number in your request. Accounting verifies it before your request moves forward.

Are all documents paid?

No — a Statement of Accounting is free and skips the payment step entirely. A Diploma - Affidavit of Loss has no published rate and is assessed by the issuing office instead.

How do I get proof of payment?

Once Accounting verifies your payment, an official E-Receipt is generated automatically and can be downloaded from your account any time.

Tracking & Pickup
How do I track my request?

Sign in and open "My Requests" to see a live status timeline for each request, from submission through release.

How long does processing take?

Documents are processed within 7 working days after payment verification, depending on the document and department. Processing days exclude Sundays and official Philippine holidays.

How do I claim my document?

Once your request reaches "Release," download the Release Slip and bring it — along with a valid school or government ID — to the issuing office.
